Tapestrague

Tapestrague is a 2D side-scrolling puzzle game in the style of a medieval tapestry. Players are trapped as a knight in a tapestry slowly decaying from a mold "plague," talking with the characters stitched into each scene and finding the pieces to solve the puzzles that open the way to the next section. The art is sourced from and inspired by public domain medieval tapestries, with a tone closer to Monty Python than authentic medieval depiction.

I'm leading development at Yale studio Amoriem Labs as project lead and design lead, heading a team of about 25 across programming, art, story, music and sound, and design. With about 20 weeks of active development and fewer than 19 meetings before an April 2027 launch, the production is built to keep a volunteer team moving: weekly meetings that open with a group playtest, team leads for each discipline, and tasks tracked in Notion and sent out in weekly emails.

The game is in early prototyping: mouse-based image-completion puzzles (medieval dials, sliding blocks, and grid-connection puzzles), click-to-move side-scrolling, dialogue, and rigged characters in photo-bashed tapestry backgrounds.
